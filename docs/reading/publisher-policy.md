# Reader context and connected coverage

Approved September 13, 2026 after private comparison against September 12.

For each future edition, prepare reviewed entries in `_data/reading-support.json`, keyed by date and item ID. The generator renders the same entry on the edition and permanent item page.

- Add one short explanation only when it helps understand the item. Explain a concept; do not introduce unverified product claims.
- Choose New development, Update, or Background and explain why. These are editorial roles, not evidence-strength or availability ratings. A new topic alone does not prove a new development.
- Select earlier Brief coverage only when the relationship is useful. Check the actual archived page and date. Include a sentence explaining the connection. Omit weak matches; never imply related coverage substantiates a claim it does not address.
- For a contrasting perspective use a descriptive heading, such as “Related perspective: practical AI risks.” The September 12 podcast connection explicitly does not substantiate the headline’s probability.
- Reading estimates use 200 words per minute for the summary, significance, practical step, and context note, rounded up. Linked reading, books, images, and media playback are excluded. Video/podcast duration comes from verified runtime_seconds; missing or invalid duration stays unverified.
- The renderer never invents a label, explanation, or related item when an editorial entry is absent. It can still calculate reading time or display recorded media duration. Review absence in publishing QA.
- Preserve all existing summaries, evidence qualifications, book references, links, rating identities, and share controls. Use normal validation and atomic publication gates.
