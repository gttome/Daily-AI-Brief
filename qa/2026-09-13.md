### First-pass failures and repairs

The first post-commit suite exposed a stale hard-coded date in the daily-feed test. The test now derives the latest staged edition date, and all 107 tests plus 15 contract fixtures pass.

Independent live QA then found that article ratings sent an extra `x-rating-revision` header and advertised same-session changes outside the required transport. PR #89 removed that header and change path, added regression assertions, and deployed matching JavaScript bytes. The final client sends only `brief_date`, `item_id` and `rating` in the body, uses `x-operation-id`, retains that ID across retry, stops retries after 29 days, and accepts `recorded:true` without exposing a rating count.

No unresolved Critical or High defect remains. The correction ledger was not changed because both repairs were mechanical and did not alter story facts.
